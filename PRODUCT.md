# Keif Electric

## Register
brand

Marketing site for a one-person residential electrical contractor. Design is the product: it has to earn a phone call or a quote request from a homeowner who has never heard of Adam.

## Users
Homeowners in San Luis Obispo County (San Luis Obispo, Atascadero, Paso Robles, Los Osos, Templeton and nearby) with a specific need: a panel that is full or tripping, an EV charger to install, lighting for a remodel, or something that stopped working. Most arrive on a phone from Google search or the Google Business Profile, often comparing two or three electricians. Secondary: trades partners (GCs, realtors, home inspectors) checking that Adam is licensed and real.

## Product purpose
Get the visitor to call, text, or submit a quote request, and make Adam look like the licensed, careful, owner-operated electrician he is. Nothing else. No blog, no ecommerce, no account.

## Brand personality
Trustworthy and competent. Plain-spoken first person ("I quote the job, I do the work, and I answer the phone"). Finish-quality craftsmanship. Warm, sun-baked Central Coast palette: ink, bone, orange, rust. Confident but never salesy.

## Anti-references
- Franchise electrician sites: stock photos of smiling models, "24/7 emergency" banners, coupon pop-ups, chat widgets.
- Generic SaaS aesthetics: gradients, glass cards, hero-metric rows.
- Doorway-page SEO sites: keyword-stuffed headings, thin city pages.
- Em dashes and exclamation points in copy (house rule).
- Stock imagery presented as Adam's work. Only real job photos and Adam's own photo.

## Strategic design principles
1. Phone first. Click-to-call is one tap from every screen; the quote form is the backup for people who will not call right now.
2. Proof over claims. License number, insurance, real reviews with names, real job photos.
3. Legible before stylized. Climate Crisis is the brand voice for hero and CTA moments; Geist carries anything a visitor has to read to decide.
4. Mobile is the primary surface. Roughly three quarters of traffic.
5. Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast on all text and controls (audited and fixed 2026-08-04).
